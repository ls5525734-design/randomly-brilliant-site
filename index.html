Randomly Brilliant Uploader

A small, private desktop tool used by the team behind the Randomly Brilliant channel to send the channel's own short educational videos to the channel's own TikTok account.

Not a public service. The tool is not offered, sold or distributed to anyone else. It connects only to TikTok accounts owned by the operator of Randomly Brilliant.

What it does

  • Picks up short videos that the channel has already produced, fact-checked and approved.
  • Checks that each video file is complete and in a format TikTok accepts.
  • Prepares a short caption and a few relevant hashtags from the video's existing title and description.
  • Uploads the video to the TikTok account's inbox through TikTok's official Content Posting API. The account owner then opens the TikTok app, reviews the draft, chooses its settings and decides whether to post it.
  • Checks the upload status and keeps a local record of each upload, so that no video is sent twice.

What it does not do

  • It does not access any account other than the operator's own.
  • It does not read messages, followers, analytics or any other account data.
  • It does not add watermarks or promotional overlays to videos.
  • It does not log in for the user or bypass any TikTok control. Authorization uses TikTok's official Login Kit, and access can be withdrawn at any time from TikTok's app permission settings.

About the channel

Randomly Brilliant publishes short, curious and slightly sarcastic explainers about science, history and everyday mysteries. You can find it on YouTube.
